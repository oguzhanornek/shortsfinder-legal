Delete your Shorts Finder account

Shorts Finder is developed by Oğuzhan Örnek. You can ask us to delete your account and the data linked to it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to ornekoguzhan@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Shorts Finder account".
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to the app, so we can confirm the account is yours.
  3. We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (user ID, email, name)
  • Your projects, transcripts, suggested moments and settings
  • Any uploaded media still in storage (it is deleted automatically within 3 days anyway)
  • Your unused credit balance (credits are not refunded on deletion)

What may be kept

Records of purchases (date, pack, amount) may be kept for up to 10 years where tax or accounting law requires it. Videos you exported stay on your phone; delete them from your gallery if you wish.
