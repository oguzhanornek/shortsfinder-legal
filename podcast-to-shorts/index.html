How to turn a podcast episode into Shorts

Updated 18 October 2026

The quickest way: paste the episode’s YouTube link into ShortsFinder. The AI goes through the whole conversation, ranks the most quotable moments and turns the ones you pick into captioned 9:16 Shorts, ready to post.

Why podcasts make great Shorts

A one-hour episode usually holds several moments that stand on their own: a bold opinion, a story with a payoff, a funny exchange, a piece of advice people will want to save. Clipped and captioned, each of them can reach listeners who would never start a full episode, and bring some of them back to it.

What the AI looks for in a conversation

  • Strong statements: a clear opinion said in one or two sentences.
  • Story payoffs: the end of an anecdote, where the point lands.
  • Reactions: laughter, surprise, a guest who pushes back.
  • Practical takeaways: a tip or insight that makes sense without the rest of the episode.

Every moment gets a score, so you can start with the strongest ones. Because ShortsFinder also watches the picture, it notices reactions on camera, not only what’s said.

Step by step

  1. Paste the episode’s YouTube link, or pick the video from your gallery.
  2. Wait for the analysis and look through the ranked moments.
  3. Pick your Shorts. Each analysis includes 3; extras cost a few credits.
  4. Choose a caption style, then trim and reframe so the speaker stays in the 9:16 frame.
  5. Export in 1080p and post on YouTube Shorts, TikTok or Instagram Reels.

Tips for podcast clips

  • Start on the sentence that matters, not on the question that led to it.
  • Keep each clip to one thought; 20 to 60 seconds is usually enough.
  • Captions matter even more for talking clips, because many people watch with the sound off.
  • Put the guest’s name or the topic in the title so new viewers know who’s talking.

FAQ

Can I clip an audio-only podcast?

ShortsFinder works with videos: a YouTube link or a video file from your gallery. Many podcasts publish a video version on YouTube, and that works well.

How many Shorts can I get from one episode?

Each analysis includes 3 Shorts of your choice, and you can pick more moments from the same analysis for a few extra credits.

Are the captions automatic?

Yes. Captions are generated word by word from the speech, and you can choose from five styles.
